# Game Studio 42 — Contributor License Agreement Policy

**Draft for legal review — not yet in force.**

Game Studio 42 welcomes contributions to open-source games and game-development tools. Contributors retain copyright in their original contributions. Before a contribution is merged, the contributor must sign the applicable Individual CLA (ICLA) and, where an employer or other entity owns relevant rights, the Corporate CLA (CCLA).

The CLA grants the project steward permissions to reproduce, modify, distribute, sublicense and relicense contributions, including commercially, while maintaining an open-source edition. It does not transfer copyright or grant contributors rights to official project trademarks. Signing does not guarantee acceptance of a contribution.

The legal identity of the project steward, effective date, signatory process, and treatment of existing contributions must be finalized before this policy is enforced. See [ICLA](ICLA.md), [CCLA](CCLA.md), and [workflow](docs/CLA_WORKFLOW.md).
