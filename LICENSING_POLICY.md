# Game Studio 42 Licensing Policy

**Proposed organization policy — subject to review and adoption.**

- Complete games: **AGPL-3.0-or-later** for original game code, unless a repository explicitly documents an exception.
- Independently reusable libraries and tools: generally **Apache-2.0** or **MIT**, with compatibility reviewed before integration.
- Documentation: generally **CC BY 4.0**.
- Original artwork, models, music and sound: explicitly licensed per project and asset; do not assume the code license applies to every asset.
- Third-party dependencies and assets retain their own licenses and must be recorded in an asset/dependency register.
- Official project names, logos and marks are governed separately by the [Trademark Policy](TRADEMARK_POLICY.md).

AGPL permits commercial use and redistribution subject to its terms. Alternative commercial licensing is only possible where the relevant rights are held or validly granted; the CLA is intended to establish those permissions for future accepted contributions. Existing contributions must be audited independently. This policy does not retroactively relicense third-party works.
